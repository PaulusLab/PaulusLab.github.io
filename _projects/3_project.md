A full-stack research management platform built for lab-wide use, tracking projects, tasks, conferences, grants, and lab resources in one place. Built with Next.js and PostgreSQL, with a mobile-optimized interface for use on the go.

The dashboard is used internally by lab members and is not publicly accessible.
