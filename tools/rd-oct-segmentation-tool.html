RD OCT Segmentation Tool

Upload a retinal OCT B-scan image to segment four biomarkers relevant to retinal detachment: subretinal fluid, outer/intraretinal cysts, and epiretinal membrane. Built on a SegFormer-B4 model trained by the Paulus Lab.

⚠ Research Prototype — Not for Clinical Use
Drag and drop an OCT B-scan image, or click to browse
PNG or JPG, single B-scan image
Running segmentation — first request may take up to a minute while the model wakes up.
This tool is a research prototype developed by the Paulus Lab at Johns Hopkins University. It is not FDA-cleared and should not be used to guide clinical decisions without physician oversight. Model code, training details, and full performance numbers: github.com/PaulusLab/rd-oct-segmentation-model.
